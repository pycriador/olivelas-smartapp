## What the AIOps team is

AIOps is the team that builds and maintains Loft's internal data and productivity applications — things like project platforms, data reconciliation, and quality tooling that other squads use day to day. It isn't the team that runs infrastructure itself (that's a separate platform team); AIOps builds products **on top of** that infrastructure.

## Who this hub is for

This Confluence space exists for three audiences:

- **People from other areas** who use or depend on one of our apps and want to quickly understand what it does and who to reach.
- **New team members** arriving and needing an overview before diving into technical detail.
- **Technical team members and AI agents**, who use the specification pages (denser, table-driven, evidence-tagged) as the source of truth for architecture, integrations, and operations.

## Why it matters

AIOps applications (cataloged in [applications.md](applications.md)) serve internal Loft squads every day. When one of these systems goes down, the impact lands on whoever depends on it to close reconciliations, look up projects, or validate data quality — that's why we keep public documentation of "what each thing is" and "who to talk to," on top of the purely technical material.

## How the content is organized

This hub follows a three-level model: **team** (this level — who we are and how we work), **application** (one page per product, with goal, architecture, and integrations), and **repository** (the technical README of each project on GitHub, with run/development detail). Every application in the catalog also has, or will get, its own plain-language guide equivalent to this one.
